# Observational medicine pilot

Use the pinned public NHEFS teaching release. Freeze protocol.json, the data digest,
requirements.lock and runtime.json before analysis. Check population, denominator,
unique participants, time zero, exposure/outcome windows, missingness, confounding,
positivity and uncertainty before interpreting a contrast. Cessation timing is coarse
and overlaps outcome measurement; this protocol permits association only. Do not
present agreement between two estimators as evidence of exchangeability or clinical
benefit. Preserve negative, inconclusive and invalid results. Do not change the
verifier to match the analysis or reuse this public reproduction as hidden test data.
